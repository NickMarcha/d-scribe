Privacy

Last updated 12 October 2026.

d-scribe is a desktop app. There are no d-scribe servers and no accounts, and the app collects no usage data or crash reports. This page lists everything that leaves your PC.

Your recordings stay on your PC

Audio, transcripts, titles, notes and AI results are saved in %APPDATA%\d-scribe\recordings on your computer. Transcription runs on your computer too. You can open, move or delete these folders yourself.

Discord

d-scribe talks to the Discord app on your PC to find out which voice channel you're in, who is in it and who is speaking. To allow that, you sign in with Discord once (scopes rpc and identify). The sign-in token is stored on your PC so you don't have to sign in again. d-scribe doesn't post anything to Discord and doesn't read your messages.

Downloads and updates

The app connects to these places when it needs to:

  • GitHub, to check for d-scribe updates and to download the Whisper transcription engine
  • Hugging Face, when you download a speech recognition model

These requests contain no recordings or transcripts.

Optional features that send data elsewhere

  • Remote transcription. If you add a remote transcription service in Settings, audio of each turn is sent to the service you chose.
  • AI prompts. If you run an AI prompt on a transcript, the transcript is passed to the AI command you set up (for example Claude Code), which sends it to that AI provider.

Both are off until you set them up, and their own privacy terms apply.

Test access requests

The access form is a Google Form and needs you to sign in with Google. It asks for your Discord username, your email address and, if you want, what you'll use d-scribe for. These are used only to add you as a tester of the d-scribe Discord app and to contact you about it. To have your request removed, or to be removed as a tester, open an issue on GitHub or contact NickMarcha.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts and the form from Google Forms. Those services may log your IP address under their own privacy policies. The site sets no cookies of its own and has no analytics.

Recording other people

You decide what to record. Let the people in the call know that you record, and check the rules where you and they live.
